Questions.

Will my product photos be changed?

Your product stays your product. We use your photos as the base and add backgrounds, extra angles and looks in your brand’s style.

Do I need a designer?

No. You edit words right on the slide, or ask in plain words: “make the background lighter”.

What if my store isn’t on Shopify?

Paste a link to any product page or to your Instagram. Shopify just lets us pull everything automatically.

Where do the numbers on the slides come from?

Only from your brand: your product page, your reviews, your fitting. If a fact is missing, the slide shows a yellow note asking you for it. We never make facts up.

How do I cancel?

In Settings, in one click. Your carousels stay yours.
