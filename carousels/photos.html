Questions.

Will my piece look different?

No. Color, cut, print and details stay exactly as in your photo. Every shot is checked against it before you see it.

What photo do I need?

Any clear photo of the piece: on a hanger, laid out on the floor, on a person, from your phone. One is enough. More photos help with the back and the details.

Can I use the same model for my whole catalog?

Yes. Your model stays with your brand, so every product looks like one shoot.

What if I don’t like a shot?

Redo it, up to 3 times per shot for free. Or swap the shoes, the bag or the background and make it again.

Can I use the shots on Amazon?

Yes. We make a white-background main image for Amazon along with the rest of the set.
