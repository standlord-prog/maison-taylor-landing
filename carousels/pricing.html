Billing questions.

Is there a free trial?

Your first carousel is free, no card needed. Paste your store link and see it before you pay anything.

Do unused carousels roll over?

No, the count resets each month. You can buy an extra carousel for $12 any time.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Any time, in Settings. Changes apply from your next billing date.

How do I pay?

By card. Yearly plans are paid once a year with 20% off.
